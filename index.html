For us, it has always been about the people who walk through our doors.

American Jewelry and Loan opened in 1978 and has become one of the largest and most recognizable pawn shops in the world. The flagship Detroit location on Greenfield Road was the setting for the record-breaking reality show Hardcore Pawn, starring owners Les Gold and his son Seth.

Les is a third-generation pawnbroker. For nearly five decades, the Gold family has served Metro Detroit with the same promise: fair prices, honest appraisals, and respect for every person who walks in, whether they are pawning a watch, selling grandmother's ring, or hunting for a once-in-a-lifetime find.

Today, with five locations across Metro Detroit, the family tradition continues, and the stories our customers share are still the best part of the job.
